Our Model

Next-Gen Operations Model (Powered by AIPO)

AIPO enables a next-gen operations approach that transforms support from manual, fragmented execution to AI-led orchestration:

  • Eliminate repetitive Level 1 work through AI agents performing predefined procedures and repetitive tasks
  • Optimize Level 2 support with humans-in-the-loop handling more complex resolution
  • Shift human capital to focus on reliability and security, where judgment and governance matter most
  • Enable a turnkey orchestrator layer leveraging AI-driven observability, predictive analytics, and actionable recommendations

This model strengthens operational execution today while enabling the shift toward Agentic AI-Driven Ops over time, supported by enhanced ITSM, integrated endpoints for ITSM/telemetry/task orchestration, and integrated AI-led knowledge management.

Key outcomes include:

Predictive analytics & insights → reduces risk and error
