CAPABILITIES

Three Models. Three Moments. One Platform.

Every GCC reaches a moment where more people can't create more capacity. What you need next is a different model.

200 HC → 200 HC

Unlock hidden capacity without changing your GCC or your cost base. Same headcount, higher leverage through skill arbitrage, process efficiency, and AI-skilled talent. Humans focus on judgment, not repetition. Global coverage smooths throughput without heroics.

Relatable example: 200 HC → 200 HC. Same people. Same budget. More output. More stability. Less pressure.
200 HC → 100 HC + 100

Reset unit economics by redesigning how work happens. Value arbitrage replaces role arbitrage—outcomes delivered by humans + agents, not headcount. AAVA orchestrates work across the SDLC. Process-led delivery pods replace experience-heavy pyramids. Humans move from execution to leverage.

Relatable example: 200 HC → 100 HC + 100 agents. Fewer people. Higher output. Lower cost per outcome—permanently.
200 HC → 50 HC + 150

Move from funding capacity to purchasing outcomes. Agents absorb the bulk of volume work. AAVA runs predictable flows end-to-end with autonomous execution. Savings generated offset GCC run costs. Human oversight governs value, risk, and exceptions. Near-zero marginal cost for growth.

Relatable example: 200 HC → 50 HC + 150 agents. Less labor. More capability. Outcome funding model unlocks strategic value.
